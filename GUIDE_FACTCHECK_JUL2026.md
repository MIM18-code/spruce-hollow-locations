Full verification pass over the 12 primary + 9 secondary location cards on the live guide, plus the backing research files. Four parallel web-verification passes: film credits, 2025–26 status claims, ownership/property claims, tax incentives. ## Overall verdict

**The guide is real, not hallucinated.** Every location exists, coordinates/addresses are correct, and the core pitch of every anchor candidate verified — including the claims that looked too convenient to be true (Pence Springs' intact 1947–85 cell block and $2.2M Foxfire listing; Scotland Campus's Jan 1, 2026 Chapter 11; Quehanna's Jan 28, 2026 closure; the JCFilms one-day TALA shoot; Devon at Pennhurst; House of Cards at Crownsville; Maple Run Youth Center's existence per Maryland State Archives).

## Corrections applied (most consequential first)

1. **Fort Ritchie ownership was a decade stale.** PenMar Development Corp transferred the property to Washington County in 2016; developer John Krumpotich (JG Business Development, "Ritchie Revival") bought it April 2021 (~$1.85M). Contact path updated. Lioness filmed there **2022 (S1) and 2024 (S2)**, not "2024–25". Distance corrected ~1:00 → ~1:25.
2. **Mt. Savage School card INVALIDATED.** MLS #MDAL2014220 (17006 Mount Savage Rd NW) is a 1992 single-family house, not a 1920s school; Mt. Savage Elementary/Middle are active. Card marked do-not-scout; St. Michael's Frostburg covers the same pickup role.
3. **WV tax credit is transferable, NOT refundable** (WV Code §11-13X). 27% + 4% (10+ WV residents) = 31%, $50K min, no cap — all correct — but the credit is sold at a discount, not paid as cash. Also: **program sunsets Dec 31, 2027**. All "31% refundable" language corrected across cards, scenario decks, and incentives section. Ohio (30%) is the region's only refundable credit.
4. **Quehanna disposition path corrected.** Closure date (Jan 28, 2026) confirmed, but land is owned by **PA DCNR** — no DGS surplus listing for Quehanna verified (DGS was reported handling Rockview, not Quehanna). Closure driver was occupancy (89/612 beds) and cost, not the missing fence. First call must establish the controlling agency.
5. **Silence of the Lambs removed from WV Pen Moundsville** — tourism myth; those scenes shot around Pittsburgh (Western Center, Canonsburg). Fool's Parade, Out of the Furnace, and Castle Rock credits verified. **WV Pen confirmed open and touring as of July 2026** — the Jan 2026 "temporarily closed" flag was seasonal.
6. **MD DECADE Act (HB0898) signed May 12, 2026 (Ch. 352).** Amber "awaiting signature" warning replaced. Vote counts (113-23 / 46-1, Apr 13) verified. The "June 30, 2026 sunset" was actually the FY26 certification cap, not a statutory sunset; the act also eliminates the $10M per-production cap. FY26 pool is $12M (a $20M figure in one research file was from an unenacted draft).
7. **Rowlesburg School is 1987 construction** (post-1985-flood replacement), not "1930s brick" — the guide card contradicted the repo's own `LOCATIONS_CLOSED_SCHOOLS.md`. Card corrected; Tier 2 slot flagged for scout verification. Opportunity Center address is 200 Center St.
8. **Stale contacts replaced:** WV Film Office is Dave Lavender / wvfilm@wv.gov ("Pam Haynes Martin" left 2017, name was also wrong); OSR rentals contact is Rita Miller-Bunker, rita@mrps.org ext. 100 (no "Ashleigh Ramey" verifies); GPFO is now co-run by Erin Wagner & Nicole Shiner (Pinkenson retired Nov 2024, main line unchanged).
9. **TALA distance from Cumberland corrected in `CUMBERLAND_AREA_LOCATIONS.md`:** ~120 road miles / ~2:30, not "53 miles / 1:15" — TALA is not day-playable from a Cumberland base.
10. **Scotland Campus ownership contradiction resolved:** MSLH LLC (Bajnon, acquired 2023, Ch. 11 Jan 1 2026 in E.D.N.Y., case 26-40002) is correct; the "Winebrenner Theological Seminary" note in `NEW_LOCATION_CANDIDATES.md` reflected 2012–2017 ownership. LocationsHub listing, 1892 tunnels, and Hasidic summer camp lease all verified.
11. **Minor date/detail fixes:** Cresson acquisition announced 2019 (not 2018) and tour pricing unconfirmed (past events ~$13–40); Maple Run opened 1967, renamed 1977, **closed April 2001** (not "the 1990s"); St. Michael's Frostburg closed 2008 (not 2009); Sweet Springs auction was Nov 2015, $560K, TB sanitarium years 1942–45, Rowan Memorial Home until 1991.
